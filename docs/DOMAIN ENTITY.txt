1. THE HUMAN GROUP: PERSON, STUDENT, AND LECTURER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The foundation of the system starts with a generic human being.

* PERSON (Base Layout)
  Every person in the system automatically receives the following attributes:
  - ID
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Age
  - Nationality

* INHERITANCE (The "<|--" Arrows)
  - Definition: Solid lines with empty arrowheads pointing up from 'Lecturer' 
    and 'Student' into 'Person'.
  - Meaning: A Student IS A Person, and a Lecturer IS A Person. Both 
    automatically inherit all fields of a Person without rewriting them.

* SPECIAL FEATURES / SPECIALIZATIONS
  - Student: A unique type of person who also possesses:
    - GPA (Grades)
    - Level (e.g., Bachelor, Master)
  - Lecturer: A unique type of person who also possesses:
    - Academic Title (e.g., PhD)
    - Teaching Specialization


2. THE DEPARTMENT HUB: FACULTY AND BUILDING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section handles the physical campus structures and organizational ownership.

* FACULTY (The Main Boss)
  - Definition: Represents a department instance (e.g., Faculty of Computing).
  - Ownership: It physically owns the listings. Arrows pointing down labeled 
    "hires" and "owns" represent arrays (std::vector) that track:
    - Every Student* enrolled in that department.
    - Every Lecturer* hired to teach there.
    - Every Building* room asset it controls.

* BUILDING (A Location)
  - Holds simple structural stats:
    - roomCount
    - maxCapacity (how many people can fit inside)

* THE DASHED BACK-POINTERS
  - Definition: Dashed lines pointing back up to Faculty labeled "employedBy" 
    and "ownedBy".
  - Purpose: Allows a person or building to look backwards and reference, 
    "I belong to the Computer Science Faculty." 
  - Benefit: This two-way connection is exactly what allows the cascading 
    deletion system to work seamlessly without crashing.


3. THE SCHEDULING CORE: COURSE AND ENROLLMENTENTRY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section manages active classes and data tracking over a semester.

* COURSE (The Matchmaker)
  - Purpose: Does not permanently own people; instead, it temporarily links 
    items together for the duration of a semester.
  - To run a class, a Course holds pointers to three distinct elements:
    1. faculty: The department running/funding it.
    2. lecturer: The professor standing at the front chalkboard (taught by).
    3. building: The physical room where students sit (scheduled in).

* ENROLLMENTENTRY (How Grades are Tracked)
  - Relationship: Represented by a solid black diamond line running from 
    'Course' down to 'EnrollmentEntry' labeled "owns (linked list)".
  - Mechanism: Instead of storing a massive table of students inside the 
    course, a Course points to a small link chain called an EnrollmentEntry.
  - Data Structure: Each link card in this chain holds:
    - Student's ID
    - Final Grade
    - Pointer to the next card (Linked List)
  - Connection: Uses a dashed line ("references via ID") to link that grade 
    back to the actual Student profile.
