1. THE PERSONMANAGER TRIGGER PIPELINE (The Top-Down Flow)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trigger: Actioned when calling 'deletePerson(personId)' 
         (e.g., a student leaves the university or a lecturer is removed).

* TO FACULTY:
  - Action: Triggers 'FacultyManager::removePersonFromFaculty()'.
  - Result: Scrubs that specific person's pointer completely out of the 
            faculty department's tracking lists.

* TO COURSE:
  - Action: Simultaneously calls 'CourseManager::removePersonFromCourseData()'.
  - Result: Automatically unenrolls the student from all active courses OR 
            cancels the lecturer's classes so no courses are left stranded 
            with a missing instructor.


2. THE FACULTYMANAGER TRIGGER PIPELINE (The Department Shutdown)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trigger: Actioned when a whole department is closed via 'deleteFaculty(faculty)'.

* TO COURSE:
  - Action: Calls 'CourseManager::removeFacultyFromCourseData()'.
  - Result: The course system searches for any classes funded or run by that 
            specific department and marks their faculty reference as 'nullptr' 
            so they aren't tied to a non-existent faculty.


3. THE BUILDINGMANAGER TRIGGER PIPELINE (The Room Closure)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trigger: Actioned when a physical campus hall is closed via 'deleteBuilding(building)'.

* TO COURSE:
  - Action: Calls 'CourseManager::removeBuildingFromCourseData()'.
  - Result: The course manager sweeps through all active timetables and clears 
            that building location pointer, changing it to 'nullptr' 
            (effectively marking it Online/None) so students aren't directed 
            to a classroom that no longer exists.
